# Internal Linking Rules

Every supporting article links upward to its pillar.

Every pillar links downward to all supporting articles.

Every cluster links horizontally to related clusters.

No article should exist without:

- one parent article
- two sibling articles
- two cross-cluster links

---

# Topical Authority Goal

Every cluster should eventually contain:

- 1 Pillar Article

- 10–20 Supporting Articles

- Original JoinNexiva Frameworks

- Checklists

- Decision Trees

- Worksheets

- Recruiter Insights

- Kenya-specific examples

- Strong internal linking

Only when a cluster reaches this standard should it be considered complete.
