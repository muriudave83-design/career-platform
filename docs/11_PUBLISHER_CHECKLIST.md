# JoinNexiva Publisher Checklist

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This checklist governs the responsibilities of the JoinNexiva Publisher.

It exists to ensure every article reaches a consistent editorial standard before being submitted to the independent QA Auditor.

The Publisher is responsible for quality.

The QA Auditor is responsible for verification.

The Publisher must complete this checklist before declaring an article ready for QA.

---

# Publishing Philosophy

Every published article should be capable of becoming the best resource available in Kenya for its topic.

The Publisher should always ask:

> "Is this the strongest article we can reasonably publish today?"

If the answer is no, improve the article before submitting it to QA.

---

# Stage 1 — Reader Value

□ The article immediately answers the reader's primary question.

□ The introduction explains what the reader will gain.

□ Every section provides practical value.

□ There is no unnecessary filler.

□ There is no repetition.

□ The article remains focused on the primary topic.

□ Advice is actionable.

□ Examples improve understanding.

□ Decision frameworks are used where appropriate.

□ Checklists are included where they genuinely help.

---

# Stage 2 — Editorial Quality

□ Exactly one H1 exists.

□ Heading hierarchy is correct.

□ Section order is logical.

□ Paragraphs are concise.

□ Lists improve readability.

□ Tables improve comprehension.

□ Callouts add genuine value.

□ Recruiter insights are evidence-based.

□ Kenya-specific context is maintained throughout.

□ Tone matches JoinNexiva standards.

---

# Stage 3 — SEO

□ Search intent is fully covered.

□ Primary keyword appears naturally.

□ Related keywords are covered.

□ Headings are descriptive.

□ Internal links are relevant.

□ The article supports its content cluster.

□ The article strengthens topical authority.

□ Meta description matches article content.

□ Frontmatter accurately reflects the article.

---

# Stage 4 — MDX Quality

□ Frontmatter is valid.

□ Required fields are complete.

□ Markdown renders correctly.

□ Tables are valid.

□ Lists are valid.

□ Internal links use the correct URL structure.

□ Supported MDX components only.

□ No unsupported syntax exists.

---

# Stage 5 — Reader Experience

□ Article scans well.

□ Important information appears early.

□ Large sections are broken appropriately.

□ Reading flow is natural.

□ Formatting is consistent.

□ Mobile readability has been considered.

---

# Stage 6 — Publishing Readiness

□ Article fits the roadmap.

□ Internal links point to existing or planned articles.

□ No factual inconsistencies remain.

□ No placeholder content exists.

□ No unfinished sections remain.

□ The article represents the JoinNexiva brand.

---

# Final Publisher Decision

The Publisher must answer the following question honestly.

Can I confidently publish this article on JoinNexiva today without further editorial work?

If YES:

Submit the article to the independent QA Auditor.

If NO:

Improve the article before QA.
