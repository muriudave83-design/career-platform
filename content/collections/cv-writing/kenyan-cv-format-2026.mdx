# Kenyan CV Format (2026): The Complete Guide

A well-written CV can still be rejected if it is poorly formatted.

Recruiters often review dozens—or even hundreds—of applications for a single vacancy. A CV that is difficult to scan, inconsistent or cluttered creates unnecessary work and can reduce your chances of progressing to the interview stage.

Good formatting does not make you more qualified.

It makes your qualifications easier to understand.

The best CV formats guide recruiters naturally from one section to the next, highlighting your most relevant strengths without distractions.

This guide explains the professional CV format most commonly expected by Kenyan employers in 2026. Whether you're applying for an internship, graduate trainee programme or experienced role, you'll learn how to structure your CV for readability, professionalism and Applicant Tracking System (ATS) compatibility.

---

## Quick Answer

The best CV format in Kenya is clean, professional and easy to scan. Begin with your contact information and professional summary, followed by education, experience, projects, skills, certifications and other relevant sections. Use consistent formatting, standard fonts and clear headings to ensure both recruiters and Applicant Tracking Systems can read your CV easily.

---

## Quick Formatting Checklist

Before sending your CV, confirm that:

- The layout is clean and uncluttered.
- There is exactly one consistent font family.
- Font sizes are consistent.
- Headings are clearly separated.
- Margins are even.
- Dates follow the same format throughout.
- Bullet points are aligned.
- Spacing is consistent.
- The document is saved as PDF unless instructed otherwise.
- The filename is professional.

Small formatting improvements can significantly improve readability.

## Why CV Formatting Matters

Recruiters spend very little time reviewing each application during the first screening.

Your CV should therefore answer two questions immediately:

1. Can I find the information I need quickly?
2. Does this candidate appear professional?

Poor formatting slows recruiters down.

Good formatting helps them focus on your qualifications instead of your document.

> **Recruiter Insight**
>
> Recruiters rarely reject candidates because of font choice alone. They reject CVs because poor formatting hides relevant information, creates confusion or suggests a lack of attention to detail.

## The Ideal Order of Sections

Most professional Kenyan CVs follow a logical structure.

The exact order may vary depending on your experience, but this layout works well for most applicants.

| Order | Section |
|--------|---------|
| 1 | Contact Information |
| 2 | Professional Summary |
| 3 | Education |
| 4 | Professional Experience |
| 5 | Internships / Industrial Attachment |
| 6 | Projects |
| 7 | Technical Skills |
| 8 | Certifications |
| 9 | Leadership & Volunteer Experience |
| 10 | Awards & Achievements |
| 11 | Referees (if requested) |

If you're a student or recent graduate with limited employment history, move Projects and Leadership above Professional Experience to emphasise your strongest qualifications.

## Contact Information

Place your contact information at the very top of the first page.

Include:

- Full name.
- Phone number.
- Professional email address.
- LinkedIn profile (if complete).
- Portfolio or GitHub (where relevant).

Avoid including:

- National ID number.
- Religion.
- Tribe.
- Marital status.
- Height.
- Weight.
- Passport photograph unless specifically requested.

Modern recruitment focuses on your qualifications rather than unnecessary personal details.

---

## Professional Summary

Immediately below your contact details, include a concise professional summary.

This section should introduce who you are, your qualifications and the value you bring.

Keep it between three and five sentences.

Avoid generic statements.

Instead, explain:

- Your current qualification.
- Your strongest skills.
- Your career interests.
- The type of opportunity you're seeking.

For graduates with no formal work experience, your summary should focus on potential rather than employment history.

## Choosing the Right Font

Your font should make your CV easy to read.

Recruiters care far more about readability than creativity.

Recommended fonts include:

| Font | Suitable? | Notes |
|------|:---------:|------|
| Calibri | ✅ | Modern and widely accepted |
| Aptos | ✅ | Microsoft's current default font |
| Arial | ✅ | Clean and highly readable |
| Helvetica | ✅ | Professional appearance |
| Cambria | ✅ | Excellent for printed CVs |
| Georgia | ✅ | Good for text-heavy documents |

Avoid decorative fonts such as:

- Comic Sans
- Papyrus
- Brush Script
- Curlz MT
- Jokerman

These reduce professionalism and distract from your qualifications.

> **Career Coach Tip**
>
> Recruiters should remember your achievements—not your font choice. A simple font keeps the focus where it belongs.

---

## Font Size Guidelines

Consistency is more important than using large text.

The following sizes work well for most CVs.

| Element | Recommended Size |
|----------|-----------------:|
| Name | 18–22 pt |
| Section Headings | 13–14 pt |
| Body Text | 10–12 pt |
| Contact Information | 10–11 pt |

Avoid reducing the font simply to fit more information onto one page.

Instead, remove content that does not strengthen your application.

---

## Margins and White Space

White space makes your CV easier to scan.

Crowded pages often discourage recruiters from reading carefully.

A good starting point is:

| Setting | Recommendation |
|----------|---------------|
| Margins | 1 inch (2.54 cm) or slightly smaller |
| Line Spacing | 1.0–1.15 |
| Paragraph Spacing | Consistent throughout |
| Alignment | Left aligned |

Resist the temptation to fill every available space.

A well-balanced layout appears more professional.

---

## Use Clear Section Headings

Section headings guide recruiters through your CV.

Common headings include:

- Professional Summary
- Education
- Professional Experience
- Projects
- Skills
- Certifications
- Leadership Experience
- Volunteer Experience
- Awards
- Referees

Use the same formatting for every heading.

For example:

- Bold
- Slightly larger font
- Consistent spacing above and below

Consistency improves readability.

---

## Bullet Points vs Long Paragraphs

Recruiters scan before they read.

Bullet points make important information easier to identify.

Instead of writing:

> During my internship I was responsible for supporting users, documenting issues, maintaining computers and assisting with network troubleshooting while learning from senior technicians.

Write:

- Supported first-line technical support.
- Documented recurring technical issues.
- Assisted with workstation deployment.
- Participated in routine network maintenance.
- Worked alongside senior ICT technicians.

The second version is easier to scan and understand.

## One-Page or Two-Page CV?

This is one of the most common questions among job seekers.

The answer depends on your experience.

| Experience Level | Recommended Length |
|------------------|-------------------|
| Student | One page |
| Recent Graduate | One page |
| Early Career (1–5 years) | One to two pages |
| Experienced Professional | Two pages |

Do not add unnecessary information simply to reach two pages.

Quality is always more important than length.

> **Recruiter Insight**
>
> Recruiters rarely reject a CV because it has two pages. They reject CVs because the second page contains information that adds little value.

## Chronological, Functional or Combination Format?

There are three common CV structures.

### Reverse Chronological CV

This is the format most employers expect.

It lists your most recent education and experience first.

**Best for:**

- Most applicants.
- Students.
- Graduates.
- Professionals with steady career progression.

---

### Functional CV

A functional CV focuses on skills instead of employment history.

It may be appropriate when:

- Changing careers.
- Returning to work after a long break.
- Experience is highly fragmented.

However, many recruiters prefer chronological CVs because employment history is easier to follow.

---

### Combination CV

A combination CV blends both approaches.

It highlights skills while still presenting employment history in chronological order.

This works well for professionals with several years of experience or specialised expertise.

---

## Which Format Should Most Kenyans Use?

For the majority of applicants—including students, graduates and professionals—the reverse chronological format remains the strongest option.

It is:

- Familiar to recruiters.
- ATS friendly.
- Easy to read.
- Easy to update.
- Appropriate across industries.

Unless you have a specific reason to choose another structure, this should be your default format.

## ATS-Friendly Formatting

Many employers use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to organise applications.

An ATS works best when your CV uses simple formatting.

Follow these guidelines:

### Use

- Standard section headings.
- Black text.
- Left alignment.
- Editable text.
- Consistent spacing.
- Standard fonts.

### Avoid

- Text boxes.
- Decorative graphics.
- Multiple columns where possible.
- Excessive icons.
- Images used for important information.
- Complex page layouts.

An ATS-friendly CV is also easier for recruiters to read.

---

## File Format

Unless the employer requests another format, submit your CV as a PDF.

Advantages include:

- Preserves formatting.
- Opens consistently across devices.
- Prevents accidental editing.
- Appears more professional.

Only submit a Word document if the employer specifically requests one.

---

## Professional File Naming

Never upload a file named:

- CV Final Final.docx
- New CV 7.pdf
- Updated Resume Latest.pdf

Instead, use a consistent naming convention.

Examples:

- Brian_Mwangi_CV.pdf
- Faith_Achieng_CV.pdf
- Kevin_Omondi_CV.pdf

Professional file names create a stronger first impression before the recruiter even opens your CV.

> **Hiring Manager Perspective**
>
> Small details communicate professionalism. A clear filename suggests organisation and attention to detail, while confusing filenames can create an unnecessary negative impression.

## Common CV Formatting Mistakes

Many applicants lose interview opportunities because their CV is difficult to read rather than because they lack qualifications.

Fortunately, these mistakes are easy to avoid.

### Using Too Many Fonts

Choose one professional font family and use it consistently.

Changing fonts throughout your CV creates an untidy appearance and distracts recruiters.

---

### Inconsistent Spacing

Uneven spacing between headings, paragraphs and bullet points makes a CV appear rushed.

Before submitting your CV, quickly scroll through the document and check that spacing is consistent from beginning to end.

---

### Overusing Bold, Italics and Colours

Formatting should guide the reader—not compete for attention.

Use bold only for:

- Section headings.
- Job titles.
- Institution names where appropriate.

Avoid highlighting large blocks of text or using multiple colours unless specifically required for a design-related role.

---

### Large Blocks of Text

Recruiters scan before they read.

If a paragraph contains more than four or five lines, consider breaking it into:

- Bullet points.
- Smaller paragraphs.
- Tables where appropriate.

Improving readability makes important information easier to find.

---

### Poor Date Formatting

Dates should follow one consistent style throughout the CV.

For example:

**Correct**

- Jan 2024 – Aug 2024
- 2023 – 2025
- September 2022 – Present

Avoid mixing formats such as:

- 01/03/24
- March 2024
- 2024-03
- 03-2024

Consistency reflects professionalism.

---

### Misaligned Content

Ensure that:

- Bullet points line up correctly.
- Tables are aligned.
- Dates appear consistently.
- Headings begin in the same position.

Small alignment issues may seem insignificant, but together they affect the overall presentation of your CV.

# Frequently Asked Questions

## Which CV Format Do Most Kenyan Employers Prefer?

Most employers prefer a clean reverse chronological CV because it is familiar, easy to read and compatible with Applicant Tracking Systems.

---

## Should I Use Colour on My CV?

For most professions, black text on a white background remains the safest and most professional choice.

Applicants in creative industries may use subtle design elements, but readability should always come first.

---

## Is a Two-Column CV Recommended?

Simple single-column layouts are generally the safest option because they are easier for recruiters and ATS software to process.

Only use more complex layouts if you are confident they will not reduce readability.

---

## Should My CV Be Designed Like an Infographic?

No.

Infographic-style CVs often prioritise appearance over clarity and may not perform well with Applicant Tracking Systems.

Professional presentation is more important than visual decoration.

---

## Can I Use a CV Template?

Yes.

However, customise every template to reflect your own qualifications and ensure the formatting remains clean and professional.

Avoid leaving placeholder text or unnecessary design elements.

---

## How Often Should I Update My CV?

Review your CV regularly.

Update it whenever you:

- Complete a project.
- Earn a certification.
- Finish an internship.
- Gain new responsibilities.
- Achieve a significant milestone.

Keeping your CV current saves time when new opportunities arise.
