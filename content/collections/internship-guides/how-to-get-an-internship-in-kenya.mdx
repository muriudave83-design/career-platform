# How to Get an Internship in Kenya

Getting your first internship can feel difficult, especially when many opportunities ask for experience. The good news is that employers are often looking for potential, enthusiasm, and a willingness to learn—not just an extensive CV.

## Start Preparing Early

Don't wait until your final semester.

Begin by:

- Building your CV
- Creating a professional LinkedIn profile
- Practising interview questions
- Following companies you admire

## Use Multiple Sources

Don't rely on one website.

Look at:

- Company career pages
- University career offices
- JoinNexiva
- LinkedIn
- Professional associations

## Tailor Every Application

Avoid sending the same CV to every employer.

Read the job description carefully and customise your:

- CV
- Cover letter
- Skills section

## Build Experience

Experience doesn't only come from internships.

You can also:

- Volunteer
- Complete online projects
- Contribute to open-source
- Join university clubs

## Stay Consistent

Finding an internship is a numbers game.

Apply consistently while improving your skills every week.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When should I start applying?

Ideally 3–6 months before your desired start date.

### Can I get an internship without experience?

Yes. Many employers hire students based on potential, projects, leadership, and communication skills.

### Where can I find internship opportunities?

JoinNexiva, company websites, universities, LinkedIn, NGOs, and government organisations.

## Final Thoughts

Your first internship won't define your entire career, but it can open the door to future opportunities. Focus on learning, improving, and applying consistently.
