# JoinNexiva Content Strategy
Version: 1.0

---

# Mission

Build the most trusted early-career knowledge platform in Africa by publishing authoritative, accurate and practical content that genuinely helps students and graduates.

Content exists to:

- Solve real career problems.
- Build topical authority.
- Earn trust.
- Generate organic traffic.
- Convert readers into JoinNexiva users.

Traffic is a by-product of trust.

---

# Publishing Priorities

Always publish in this order:

1. Pillar article.
2. Supporting articles.
3. Link supporting articles back to the pillar.
4. Update the pillar with links to new supporting articles.

Never publish isolated articles.

Every article must belong to a content cluster.

---

# Topic Clusters

Priority order:

1. Internship Guides
2. CV Writing
3. Interview Preparation
4. Graduate Programmes
5. Career Growth
6. Company Insights

Complete one cluster before moving deeply into the next.

---

# Search Intent Categories

Every article must target one primary intent:

- Informational
- Navigational
- Commercial Investigation

Do not mix multiple primary intents into one article.

---

# Internal Linking Rules

Each supporting article should include:

- Link to its pillar article.
- Links to 2–4 related supporting articles (where available).
- Link to relevant JoinNexiva listings or category pages when appropriate.

Each pillar article should be updated whenever a new supporting article is published.

---

# Quality Over Quantity

Do not publish to hit a number.

Only publish articles that are genuinely useful.

If an article does not improve on what already exists online, rewrite it.

---

# Kenya First

Primary examples should come from:

- Kenyan employers.
- Kenyan universities.
- Kenyan hiring practices.
- Kenyan labour market.

Expand to East Africa only when strategically appropriate.

---

# Updating Content

Review pillar articles at least every 6 months.

Review supporting articles annually or whenever there is a significant change in hiring practices, regulations or opportunities.

Update the `updated` frontmatter field whenever meaningful changes are made.

---

# Editorial Review

Every article must pass editorial review before publication.

Target score:

95+/100

If the score is below 95, revise before publishing.

---

# Success Metrics

Track:

- Indexed pages.
- Organic clicks.
- Organic impressions.
- Average position.
- Click-through rate (CTR).
- Articles ranking in the top 10.
- Articles earning featured snippets.
- Internal link coverage.

Success is measured by reader value first, rankings second.
