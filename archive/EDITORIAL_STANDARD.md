# JoinNexiva Editorial Standard v1.0

## Mission

Help Kenyan students, graduates and young professionals launch successful careers through accurate, trustworthy and practical career guidance.

Every article must satisfy Google's Helpful Content guidelines and E-E-A-T principles.

---

# Standard Structure

Every article follows this order.

1. Frontmatter
2. Hero Title
3. Introduction
4. Key Takeaways
5. Table of Contents
6. Main Sections
7. Expert Tips
8. Common Mistakes
9. Frequently Asked Questions
10. Related Resources
11. CTA
12. Author

---

# Writing Style

Write for humans first.

Professional.

Friendly.

Practical.

Never use AI clichés.

Avoid:

- "In today's competitive landscape..."
- "Unlock your potential..."
- "Game changer..."

Use:

- Clear explanations
- Real Kenyan examples
- Actionable advice

---

# Reading Level

Target:

Grade 8–10 English.

Short paragraphs.

Short sentences.

Lots of headings.

Bullets where useful.

---

# SEO Rules

Every article must contain:

One H1

Multiple H2

Multiple H3

Natural keyword placement.

No keyword stuffing.

---

# Internal Links

Each article links to:

Parent pillar.

Related guides.

Relevant listings.

---

# CTA

End every article with:

Browse verified internship and graduate opportunities on JoinNexiva.

---

# Accuracy

Never invent:

Salary figures.

Government policies.

Company requirements.

Always verify factual claims before publication.

---

# Updating

Every article includes:

Published date.

Updated date.

Articles should be reviewed at least annually.

---

# Goal

Become the most trusted early-career knowledge platform in Africa.
